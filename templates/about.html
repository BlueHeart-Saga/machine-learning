About MalwareLab · AI Malware Defense

Mission & vision

We empower developers and security analysts with transparent, permission‑based machine learning to detect Android malware before it compromises devices. Our vision is an open ecosystem where every APK can be audited instantly.

99.2% precision · 0.03s inference

How it works

Static analysis of Android manifests extracts permission vectors (184 features). Ensemble models (RF, XGBoost, TabNet) vote, plus explainability via SHAP.

  • APK decompilation (AndroGuard)
  • permission2vec embedding
  • stacked ensemble (AUC 0.997)

Dataset & learning

Trained on 2.3M APK samples (50+ families). New samples trigger automated retraining pipelines with active learning feedback loops.

  • 14 malware families
  • 187 permission types
  • weekly model refresh

Trust & compliance

We follow responsible AI practices: explainability, bias mitigation, and secure handling of APK data. Infrastructure is ISO 27001 certified and GDPR ready.
