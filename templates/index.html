Frequently asked
What permissions are most dangerous?

READ_SMS, INSTALL_PACKAGES, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, RECORD_AUDIO — our model assigns weights.

Can I use my own dataset?

Absolutely. Upload CSV with at least 50 samples. We handle missing data.

Is it only for Android?

Currently optimized for Android permissions, but we plan iOS and Windows PE.

How often are models updated?

Every two weeks with new malware samples. You can also trigger retraining.

Do you offer an API?

Yes, REST API with rate limits, enterprise plans have dedicated endpoints.

What about obfuscated malware?

Our GAN-based detector spots anomalous permission combinations even if renamed.
